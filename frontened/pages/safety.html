Safety Tips & Awareness

Stay alert, stay prepared and make safety a daily habit.

🚶 Personal Safety

  • Be aware of your surroundings.
  • Avoid isolated places when possible.
  • Keep emergency contacts accessible.
  • Inform someone you trust about your plans.

📱 Digital Safety

  • Use strong passwords.
  • Never share OTPs or passwords.
  • Avoid suspicious links.
  • Keep social media accounts private.

🚗 Travel Safety

  • Plan your route before travelling.
  • Use trusted transportation services.
  • Keep your phone charged.
  • Share your travel details with someone trusted.

🆘 Emergency Awareness

  • Know your local emergency number.
  • Keep important contacts saved.
  • Identify nearby safe locations.
  • Contact emergency services if in immediate danger.
